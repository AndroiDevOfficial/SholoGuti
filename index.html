How to Play Sholo Guti

A classic strategic board game of capture, native to Southern Asia. Master the rules in 4 easy steps:

1

Setup the Grid

Each player starts with 16 beads/soldiers placed on their respective side of the board layout.

2

Alternating Moves

Players take turns moving one of their soldiers to an adjacent vacant intersection point along the grid lines.

3

Capture by Jumping

Jump directly over an opponent's adjacent soldier into an empty space right behind them to capture their bead.

4

Achieve Victory

The player who successfully captures all 16 of the opponent's soldiers wins the classic game!
