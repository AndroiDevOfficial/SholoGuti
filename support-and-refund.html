2. Refund Guidelines

As virtual coins are considered immediate consumables, purchases are generally non-refundable once delivered to your account. However, we acknowledge specific circumstances under which refunds are eligible:

  1. Accidental Purchases by Children: If an unauthorized purchase was completed by a minor without parent approval, we will gladly assist you.
  2. Failed Coin Delivery: If Google Play successfully processes your billing payment but a network connection error prevents coins from being added to your in-app profile balance, we will either manually deliver the coins or process a full refund.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, you can choose one of the following methods:

  1. Method A (Fastest) — Google Play Store Portal:
    Navigate to the Google Play Account purchase history, select Sixteen Soldiers, and tap on **"Request a Refund"** within 48 hours of purchase. Google's automated systems typically review and process these within minutes.
  2. Method B — Direct Email Support:
    Send an email to rifatahmedsajeeb@gmail.com with the subject line *"Refund Request: Coins Store"*. Please include your purchase order ID (`GPA.XXXX`) for quick validation.
